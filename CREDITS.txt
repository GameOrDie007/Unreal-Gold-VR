Unreal Gold VR: credits

Unreal Gold VR is a Game Or Die project.

Unreal Gold by Epic Games and Digital Extremes. Epic made it free; Setup downloads the original CD
from the Internet Archive when you don't have a copy. No game files are in this download.

OldUnreal's 227k patch (https://github.com/OldUnreal/Unreal-testing): the game runs on it, and Setup downloads it from
OldUnreal's GitHub when your copy doesn't have it. Not part of this download.

XOpenGLVRDrv.dll is a fork of OldUnreal's XOpenGLDrv (https://github.com/OldUnreal/XOpenGLDrv),
under the BSD 3-Clause license: LICENSE-XOpenGL.md.

openxr_loader.dll is Khronos' OpenXR loader (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK),
under the Apache License 2.0: LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt.

The Game Tome holds the game's original manuals (Unreal and Return to Na Pali), (c) Epic Games.

Thanks to the OldUnreal team for keeping the engine alive, GHWST for the Quest ports, and
Team Beef (https://www.teambeefvr.com/), whose VR ports set the pattern.

This is a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games or OldUnreal.
